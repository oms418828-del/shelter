Adopt Other Pets

Discover unique companions like reptiles, turtles, fish and more!

Tommy

Tommy is a calm 5-year-old turtle who loves basking in the sun. Great for a relaxing and educational pet experience. He enjoys fresh vegetables, shallow water for swimming, and needs a warm basking light for healthy growth.

Goldie

Goldie is a vibrant goldfish. Easy to care for and perfect for aquariums of all sizes. Goldie thrives in clean water, enjoys swimming in groups, and brings calming energy to any home space.

Iggy

Iggy is a 2-year-old iguana, calm and low-maintenance. Perfect for reptile lovers! He enjoys climbing, leafy greens, and requires a warm enclosure with UV lighting for comfort.

Harry

Harry is a small hermit crab who enjoys climbing and exploring. A fascinating companion! He prefers sandy environments, likes switching shells as he grows, and is most active at night.

Draco

Draco is a friendly bearded dragon. Loves basking and interacting with gentle handlers. He enjoys insects, vegetables, and regular human interaction. Very social and curious by nature.

Gigi

Gigi is a lively gecko. Active during evenings, perfect for observing nocturnal behavior. Gigi enjoys crickets and worms, prefers a cozy terrarium, and is easy to handle for beginners.

Alex

Alex is an adorable axolotl. Unique, aquatic, and easy to maintain in a tank. He prefers cool water, enjoys live worms or pellets, and fascinates with his ability to regrow limbs.

Shelly

Shelly loves swimming and sunbathing. Perfect for pond or aquarium enthusiasts. She enjoys leafy greens, calm environments, and is an excellent long-term pet for families.

Koi

Koi fish are peaceful and elegant, ideal for decorative ponds and aquariums. They grow beautifully with proper care, symbolize luck in many cultures, and enjoy a balanced diet.

Sam

Sam is a small salamander. Quiet, low-maintenance, and fascinating to observe. He prefers moist habitats, enjoys cool temperatures, and feeds on insects like crickets and worms.
