Adopt a Bird

Brighten your life with a feathered friend

Sunny

Sunny is a cheerful 2-year-old parakeet with a bright yellow coat. He loves singing in the mornings and enjoys being around people. Perfect for first-time bird owners looking for a playful, low-maintenance companion.

Rio

Rio is a vibrant blue-and-green macaw, 3 years old. Smart and talkative, he loves mimicking sounds and interacting with his family. He thrives in active households where he gets plenty of attention and stimulation.

Peach

Peach is a 1-year-old lovebird with a soft pink and green feather pattern. She is affectionate and bonds deeply with her caregivers. She loves perching on shoulders and chirping sweetly throughout the day.

Kiwi

Kiwi is a playful cockatiel, 2 years old. He whistles, mimics tunes, and loves head scratches. Gentle and curious, Kiwi is ideal for families and individuals who want an interactive feathered friend.

Coco

Coco is a 4-year-old African Grey parrot, one of the most intelligent bird breeds. He has a growing vocabulary and enjoys interactive play. Coco is best suited for experienced bird lovers who can provide training and engagement.
