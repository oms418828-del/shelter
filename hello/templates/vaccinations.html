💉 Animal Vaccination

Protecting pets for a healthier tomorrow

Why Vaccination Matters

Vaccines protect pets against life-threatening diseases, ensuring they live long, healthy lives. Our shelter provides safe and comprehensive vaccination services to all rescued and adopted animals.

Core Vaccines

Rabies, distemper, and parvovirus for dogs. Rabies and FVRCP for cats.

Health Records

Every vaccinated pet receives a digital health record with complete history.

Expert Care

Certified vets provide safe vaccination under expert supervision.

Recommended Vaccination Schedule

Follow this timeline for keeping pets healthy:

Age Dog Vaccines Cat Vaccines
6–8 weeks Distemper, Parvovirus FVRCP
12 weeks Rabies, Bordetella Rabies
16 weeks DHPP booster FVRCP booster
Annually Rabies booster, DHPP Rabies booster, FVRCP

Pet Care After Vaccination

Rest & Comfort

Allow your pet to rest for 24 hours to recover fully.

Hydration

Keep fresh water available at all times to aid recovery.

Monitor Health

Watch for unusual reactions and consult a vet immediately if needed.
